About College Lost & Found Portal

Streamlining item recovery and campus security assistance

The College Lost Things and Found System is an official digital initiative created to minimize loss of belongings across campus facilities including lecture halls, laboratories, library halls, canteens, and sports complexes.

Key Objectives:

  • Centralize all missing and found property reports across departments.
  • Enable transparent ownership verification through detailed proof submission.
  • Reduce administrative overhead for campus security and student welfare desks.
  • Provide real-time statistics and notification updates for students.

How It Works:

  1. Finders Report: Finder uploads photo and location of found item.
  2. Browse Directory: Students search by keyword or category.
  3. Submit Claim: Owner fills verification claim form with ownership proof.
  4. Admin Approval: Security officer verifies proof and releases the item.
