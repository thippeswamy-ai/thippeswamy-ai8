Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage student data on campus

1. Information Collection

We collect student name, roll number, department, and contact mobile number solely for the purpose of verifying item ownership and facilitating recovery of lost items on college grounds.

2. Data Usage

Personal contact details are visible to authorized campus security administrators and finders only when necessary to arrange item handovers. Mobile numbers are not published publicly to prevent spam.

3. Image Upload Storage

Uploaded photos of items and proof documents are securely stored on campus servers and deleted upon successful claim resolution or archival cycles.
